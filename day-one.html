    Day One

    You fell asleep with the TV on. The news has been running all night, and something outside is very wrong. Get out of the neighborhood alive.

    • Look around: drag to move the map, pinch to zoom.
    • Move: tap the ground. Tap a survivor first and time slows while you decide.
    • Fight: survivors swing at anything in reach on their own. Tap a zombie to go after it.
    • Guns are loud. Every shot pulls zombies from up and down the street. Guns stay holstered unless you set Guns: free or tap a zombie.
    • Scavenge: tap an item to send someone for it.
    • Dead stays dead. Nobody comes back.
