Warehouse Yard

Take the warehouse. Four hostiles are dug in behind crates inside, watching the south door. You start by the containers in the southwest corner.

  • Look around: drag with one finger to move the map. Pinch with two to zoom.
  • Select: tap a soldier to grab his fire team. Hold on a soldier to take just him. To box-select, hold on empty ground and then drag, or press Box first.
  • Move a team: tap the ground to preview where they'll stand. Tap the marker again to go. Time slows while you decide.
  • Move one soldier: a single tap sends him.
  • Suppress: pin an enemy so he barely shoots back, then flank him from the side windows or east door.
  • Grenade: one per soldier. Select one soldier, press Grenade, tap inside the circle.
  • Corners: a soldier at the edge of a wall or container leans out to shoot or throw round it. While he's leaned out, the enemy can hit him too.
  • Downed men bleed out. Park a teammate next to them to revive.
