    Loadout

    Silenced rifles for everyone. The armory has one M240, one RPG and two medic kits. A frag each.

    Night op · four missions

    Warehouse Yard

    Level 1 teaches you the squad on a small raid: knock out an enemy radio tower. Level 2: cut the power station that lights the data center. Level 3 is the big one: get into the data center across the canal and bring out the scientists.

    Controls
    • Look around: drag empty ground with one finger to move the map. Pinch with two to zoom.
    • Select: tap a team tab at the bottom, or tap a soldier to grab his fire team. Hold on a soldier, or tap his card, to take just him. To lasso, hold on empty ground and draw a loop round the soldiers you want.
    • Cards: each selected soldier's card shows his health (out of 100: green, then yellow, then orange as he's hurt, red when he's down), his weapon and his gear. Tap the gear to use it.
    • Move a team: tap the ground to preview where they'll stand. Tap the marker again to go. Time slows while you decide.
    • Stay hidden: near enemies your soldiers sneak on their own. The circle at the top fills as a hostile notices you, and turns into !! when you're spotted. Being close, in the open or in front of him gets you noticed faster. So does running near him, which he can hear through walls. Gunfire gives you away at once.
    • Move one soldier: tap him (his fire team), tap him again to take just him (or hold him, or tap his card), then tap where he should go: a single tap sends him. Dragging moves the map, never a soldier.
    • Sneak or run: the button at the bottom right shows what the selected soldiers are doing. Press it to make them do the other. They go back to automatic the next time you select them. While you're undetected they hold fire. Tap a hostile to open fire, and a squad that strikes first gets an ambush bonus.
    • Suppress: with soldiers selected, hold your finger on an enemy (or where you last saw one). They pin him down so he barely shoots back while you flank him.
    • Watch: in a fight, soldiers holding still watch the nearest enemy and fire first when one shows himself.
    • Breach: with soldiers next to a room, a BREACH marker shows at its doorway. Tap it and they stack up and go in together.
    • Grenade: one per soldier. Tap the grenade on his card, then tap inside the circle.
    • Fire: tap an enemy and everyone selected fires on him. Anyone without a clear shot moves to get one. Silenced soldiers take a man who hasn't seen them together, on a count; tap a second one during the count and they split, one each.
    • Special item: each soldier also carries one (shown on his card): smoke, a medic kit, a silencer or an RPG. Smoke blocks sight both ways. A silencer keeps his shots quiet and wears out after six. In Level 2 every rifle is silenced, and you pick the items between missions. Ammo drops refill them.
    • Corners: a soldier at the edge of a wall or container leans out to shoot or throw round it. While he's leaned out, the enemy can hit him too.
    • Doors: inside the data center the offices have doors. Walk through one and he opens it quietly; tap a door to open it and look in; BREACH at a shut door kicks it in.
    • Classes: at level 3 a soldier gets PROMOTE between missions: pick his class. Each class adds gear or a weapon, a bonus, and a skill (the third button on his card).
    • Defender: tap his build kit, then a spot: a barricade (heavy cover you shoot over; tap it with him selected to pack it up and move it). Tap the kit again for a wall that blocks sight and shots.
    • Downed men: park a teammate next to him within a minute to revive him. After that only a medic kit, or carrying him to a medical box, will save him.
