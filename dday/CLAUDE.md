# D-Day: squad tactics

Bryan's D-Day game. It began as a test level inside the horror game (Warehouse Yard, the repository's root) and was split off after Version 157: this folder has its own copy of everything (`index.html`, `css/`, `js/`, `sounds/`), and the two games share no files. A fix wanted in both has to be made in both.

- Page: `index.html` (its start card has only D-Day; the other game's buttons are kept hidden in it because the copied scripts still bind to them). Published at its own link.
- The copied horror-game scripts (levels, monsters, the HQ...) are still loaded but nothing reaches them. Stripping them out is still to do, file by file.
- No recorded voices here (`voice.js`'s loaders return at once; `sounds/` has the gunfire and effects only).
- Notes, Bryan's answers and the drafts: `docs/dday-test.md`, `docs/drafts/`. Draft tools: `tools/drafts/` (run from the scratchpad harness with `REPO` pointing at this folder).
- Working rules are the root `CLAUDE.md`'s (plain scripts in one global scope, load order set in the page, no build step; testing notes).

### The light, the sky and the colour (js/ddlight.js)
Version 188, the light, the sky and the colour (dday/js/ddlight.js; hooks: dday.js's buildDDay calls ddLightBuild(sc), ddFrame calls ddLightFrame(rdt,now)).

Why it was washed out (Version 187):
- The renderer's ACES curve at exposure 1.2 (2x inside the curve) took every bright thing to a pale cream.
- The sky was the haze colour times a white texture.
- FogExp2 0.00034 hazed the battle itself.
- The hemisphere light was nearly as strong as the sun, so shadows were faint.
- Every prop's hex colour was read as linear light, so #80 grey came out at about 80% grey.

Tone (scene.js render, D-Day only, MAPID==='dd'):
- render sets renderer.toneMapping=DDL_TONE.map (three's CustomToneMapping) and exposure DDL_TONE.exp 1.17 for D-Day's frame, and puts ACES 1.2 back after it.
- ddToneChunk (run once, at the first ddLightBuild) writes the curve into ShaderChunk.tonemapping_pars_fragment. It is straight to 0.6 linear, then a soft shoulder on the brightest channel with the others scaled with it, then a slight desaturation above 0.86.
- three r128 doesn't recompile a material when toneMapping changes. Materials compiled in D-Day frames use the custom curve and other screens keep ACES.

Grade (post.js POST.M.final):
- A dday branch, chosen by the uniform dday. Uniforms black 0.02, con 0.3 (smoothstep S-curve mix), cold 0.5 (shadows ×(0.93,0.98,1.07)), warm 0.3 (light ×(1.05,1,0.92)), sat 1.22 as vibrance (dull colours get more), vig 0.2, grain 0.01. All in ddlight.js's DDL_GRADE.
- postRender's D-Day line uses DDL_GRADE only when sc===DD.scene. The barracks room (ddmRoomFrame calls postRender directly) keeps the old day values.
- Tier 0 has no grade, only the tone curve. Checked: not washed out, a bit flatter.

Scenery colours (ddLinSweep / ddLinMat / ddLinCol):
- Lit materials (Standard, Lambert, Phong) and InstancedMesh instanceColor are taken DDL.lin 0.8 of the way from "hex as light" to the hex decoded from sRGB, so they come out as painted.
- Skipped: figure materials (userData.U), and anything with material.userData.ddKeep (the opt-out for colours already decoded).
- Runs at build and then every 0.5 s on the whole scene, to catch new objects. The sweep takes about 0.6 ms for 7,800 objects.
- Colours set later on an already-swept material are not converted, the same as in Version 187: ddGib colours, a knocked-out tank's 0x1b1a18, the captured bunkers' blue tint.

Sky table (dday.js DD_SKY, rows of [t, horizon/fog, zenith, hemi sky colour, hemi k, sun colour, sun k, sun azimuth (degrees from east toward south), elevation]):
- t=0: 0xc4ced6, 0x6f8db2, 0xb0c4da, 0.54, 0xffe8c8, 1.42, 18°, 31°.
- t=0.5: 0xccd8e2, 0x5d86ba, 0xb8cce0, 0.56, 0xfff3e0, 1.5, 62°, 46°.
- t=1: 0xdcc6aa, 0x6c80a6, 0xc8c0b8, 0.54, 0xffd2a0, 1.42, 128°, 28°.
- So the sun is from the right of the usual camera and a little behind it, golden in the morning and evening.
- ddSky fills DD.sky = {hor, zen, sunC, sun (a vector), t} and calls ddLightSky to pass it to the dome and the smoke.
- The ground texture's baked light (paintDDGround) is from the south-east, which agrees with the morning sun.

Sun shadows (ddSunFrame, every frame):
- The shadow box is fitted, in the sun's own frame, to the camera frustum's ground footprint: 9 rays, reach DDL.shadowReach 1150.
- Half-size is clamped to 170-720 in steps of 24. Casters up to 520 above the ground are included. The centre is snapped to whole texels.
- bias -0.0004, normalBias 0.7. The phone map size (1024) is unchanged.

Haze:
- sc.fog is a linear Fog in the horizon colour.
- ddLightFrame sets near = max(760, min(1700, camera distance to the view centre) × 1.25) and far = near + DDL.fogFar 3300.

Dome (ddDome):
- A ShaderMaterial on dday.js's 5200 sphere (DD.dome), centred on the camera, in display colours (no tone curve, like the fog it meets).
- Gradient mix(hor, zen, 1-exp(-h×4.2)), a pale horizon band, sun glow (pow 6 and 48), sun disc (pow 900).
- Cloud layer: the projection d.xz/(h+0.07)×0.1, three noise octaves (cl()), threshold t0 = 0.66 - cloud×0.32, lit on the sun side by a density difference toward the sun, faded into the haze near the horizon, drifting with uTime×uWind.
- DDL_MODE gives cloud and wind per chapter: beach 0.6/1, counter 0.46/1.3, village 0.7/0.8.
- The noise texture: ddNoiseTex, a cached 256² DataTexture of tiling value-noise fBm. R has period 4 and 5 octaves, G period 8 and 4 octaves, B period 16 and 3 octaves. Built once per page, about 27 ms in software. Shared by the clouds, smoke and cloud shadows.

Smoke columns (ddSmokeCols):
- DDL_COLS per mode, rows of [x, y, height, foot width, fire]. The beach has 12: 6 behind the bluffs, 4 on the next beaches, 2 out at sea. The counterattack has 8 and the village 6. All are off the play area (x 200-1200 is the battle).
- One mesh: 2 camera-facing strips per column, cut into DDL_SEG 10 so they bend.
- Width grows ×(0.42 + 2.1·y^0.8). Wind bend uWind (-0.42, 0, 0.14) × y^1.8 × height, plus a slow meander.
- Billows are noise in world-sized units, rising at 7 units/s.
- Colour: near black at the foot, dark grey in the middle, light grey at the top. Columns with tone > 0.62 are browner and paler. The sun side is lighter.
- Fire at the foot flickers when the fire flag is set. The far ones fade into the haze (uFN, uFF follow the fog).
- About 480 triangles in all.

Cloud shadows (ddCloudShade):
- The battle's own land sheet (not the mirror copies in DD.bo) is drawn a second time with MultiplyBlending.
- Noise at xz×0.0008, drifting. Strength DDL.shade 0.15 × (0.5 + cloud).
- Faded with the haze. Hidden at POST.tier 0. Men and props are not darkened.

ddlook.js ddScenery: the painted sky texture is gone (the dome replaces it). Ships, balloons and crowds are unchanged.
