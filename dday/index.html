    Normandy · June 1944

    D-Day

    Two squads of four land on the beach with the first wave. Get off the sand, up the draw and through their guns, and take the base on the top. The men you finish with are yours to keep: after the battle you see who did what, then run the base you took.

    Controls
    • Squads: tap ALPHA or BRAVO to play that squad. The other fights beside it: it moves up beside yours, pins down whoever is shooting at yours while yours runs, and when the man you're shooting is dug in it throws a grenade at him or goes round his side if nobody else can see it. The small switch on its tab says FOLLOW or HOLD: tap it to change. Tap a man's name in the bar to follow just him.
    • Move: tap the ground. Shoot: tap an enemy: your squad opens fire and a ring shows what else you can do to him (pin him down, a grenade, the bazooka). A tank or the wire tapped shows its own. Pin them: hold a finger on an enemy.
    • Tools: tap a tool in the bar (grenade, smoke, bazooka, medic kit), then where to use it. While it's in hand, the ground the man holding it can reach is orange.
    • Camera: drag to look round, pinch to zoom, twist to turn. It follows your squad, turning with the way ahead, toward where you send them when that's off the way (the first hill, a bunker to the side), and to face a tank that comes near; a drag lets go of it until your next move.
    • The fallen: a medic can bring a man back for a short while; after that a replacement comes up from the ground you hold.
