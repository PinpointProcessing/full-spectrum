# Warehouse Yard — squad tactics prototype

Combat demo for Bryan's next game after Ashvale: modern squad tactics (Full Spectrum Warrior squad control + Aliens: Dark Descent look and campaign feel) with base building and async PvP planned later. This demo only tests the battle layer.

## The game idea (long term)
- Main HQ: level up, research weapons, build defenses, store vehicles, collect resources. Modern setting, human vs human.
- Missions: overlay of enemy territory, scouting options, pick the first target, take it, then push to the next outpost/checkpoint. Captured areas become sub-bases (recruit locals, repair gear, heal the wounded, build makeshift tech). Clear the region, upgrade HQ, continue the war.
- Later: async online raids. Players build base defenses, and their forces defend on their own when attacked.
- Visual target: close to Aliens: Dark Descent.

## The demo (warehouse-yard.html)
`warehouse-yard.html` is the page shell (markup only). Three.js r128 from cdnjs (global `THREE`) renders the 3D world; a 2D canvas overlay on top draws the HUD bits (hp bars, hit %, previews, tracers, rain). No build step: open the HTML directly or serve the folder statically.

### Code layout
Styles in `css/style.css`. Game code in `js/` as plain `<script>` files (not ES modules, so it still runs from `file://`). They share one global scope, so load order matters and is set in the HTML:
1. `core.js` constants (map size, colours), DOM handles, `hyp`/`clamp`/`rng`, `teamCol`
2. `map.js` map objects (`objs`), lamp/post data, LOS, cover, free-space queries
3. `pathfinding.js` nav grid, A* + string-pulling, cover points
4. `scene.js` `initGL()` (renderer, scene, camera), camera maths (`toS`/`toW`), textures, world meshes, lights, soldier models, `render()`
5. `state.js` battle state variables, `mk`, `reset`, `timeScale`
6. `combat.js` hit chance, shots, suppression, grenades, move assignment, enemy and squad AI, `update(dt)`
7. `waves.js` the battle's second wave: phases, countdown, gun jeep, wave-two assault AI (`waveTick` from `update`, `waveHud`/`aimTurret` every frame, `resetWaves` from `reset`)
8. `hq.js` the temporary HQ after the battle: popup, camera pan, medical/supply/motor-pool zones, next-area arrow (`startHQ`, `hqTick` via `waveTick`, `drawHQ` from the overlay, `panTick` every frame)
9. `depot.js` the checkpoint fight: garrison, patrol, RPG gunner and rockets, fuel tanks, the checkpoint mission and second HQ (`startDepot` from `openArea`, `depotTick` via `waveTick`, `depotFx` every tick, `drawDepot` from the overlay, `resetDepot` from `resetWaves`)
10. `vehicle.js` the captured jeep as a squad vehicle: boarding, seats, driving on the vehicle grid, turret engagement, dismount, damage (smoke/fire/break down/explode, mines), repairs, its overlay drawing (`vehTick` from `update`, `drawVehicle` from the overlay)
11. `overlay.js` 2D overlay drawing and rain
12. `input.js` pointer/pinch/wheel handling (`bindInput`), `tap`
13. `hud.js` banner, status chip, buttons, keyboard (`bindHud`), detection ring, end screen
14. `cards.js` team tabs' health pips and the unit cards (portrait, name, status, heart monitor, equipment): `buildCards`, `updateCards` (from `hud()`), `drawCards` (every frame)
15. `progress.js` XP, levels, ranks, saving progress in localStorage, the after-action report (`showAAR`)
16. `save.js` saving the campaign at milestones and restoring it: Continue / Retry from last save / New game (`saveGame`, `continueGame`, `refreshSaveButtons`)
17. `main.js` resize, frame loop, boot (shows the no-WebGL message and stops if `initGL()` fails)

Only `main.js` does work at load time beyond building pure data (map, nav grid, cover points); everything touching THREE or wiring events runs from boot.

Scenario: rainy night, 1300x900 map (units ≈ 10 cm): the yard is x 0–600 (`YARD_W`), the shipping depot x 600–1300 opens later. 4 soldiers start in the SW corner by containers: Alpha (A1 Reyes, A2 Kowalski) and Bravo (B1 Hayes, B2 Okafor). One big warehouse in the middle with 4 hostiles behind crates watching the south roll door. Sandbag nest (U shape with single bags off each side) ahead-left. Flank routes: west window, east window and east door. Gate in the north fence (x 450–500) where reinforcements arrive. Gate at the south end of the yard's east fence (y 700–770, `depotGate`) to the depot, locked until the HQ patrol is beaten.

### Decisions made (keep unless Bryan changes them)
- Real-time with slow-mo (quarter speed) while giving orders, plus Pause. Not turn-based.
- Squad is the main unit, split into two 2-man fire teams. Individuals are for special moves (flank, grenade, revive).
- Group move = two taps: first tap previews ghost positions (green = in cover, red = exposed, from known enemies), tap the marker again to go. Single soldier moves on one tap.
- Where soldiers stand (`makeAssign`, reworked after Bryan's complaints about far-off cover and runners): spots within 14 of the tap for one soldier, 32 for two, 45 for three or four, and in line of sight of the tap (same side of a wall). Candidates are cover points plus open slots in a line across the threat direction (back row penalised). The whole group's arrangement is chosen together: cover (×2) + sees enemy (0.3) − distance from tap/12 − walk/150 − spread beyond 30. Tapping a wall/container/crate/sandbag in the 3D view targets the spot just in front of the face you tapped (`tapTarget` in scene.js, using `objHeight` from map.js), not the ground hidden behind it.
- Drawn routes for independent soldiers (Bryan, inspired by mobile squad games): drag from a soldier to draw his route. He follows the line's shape; stretches through objects are routed round (`routeFromTrail` in combat.js, dropping a dead-end bit of line if that saves 15%+ locally); the end settles on cover within 18. Quarter speed while drawing, works while paused, map edge-scrolls while drawing.
- Sneak/run toggle (Bryan's design): button bottom right (`#gaitBtn`, `toggleGait`) shows the selected soldiers' gait (AUTO SNEAK / AUTO RUN). Pressing flips them to the other gait (amber, MANUAL), pressing again returns to auto. A soldier goes back to auto when he's selected again after not being selected (`select()` in input.js; always change the selection through it). Key R.
- Weapons tight while undetected: before contact the squad only fires at a hostile you tapped (or via Suppress/grenade). Striking first is an ambush: enemies need 1.4–2 s to react and the squad gets +0.15 hit chance for 1.5 s (`alertAll(msg,true)`, `ambushing()`). The hit pill shows "· ambush" while undetected. After contact, everyone fires freely.
- Soldiers act on their own to survive: return fire, shift to cover within 50 if exposed ~2 s, but never wander off on a plan of their own. Player orders lock out auto-moves for 4 s.
- Angled 3D camera, from the south by default (pitch 60° zoomed out → 46° zoomed in), rotatable by twisting. Bryan liked this far more than top-down. Screen-relative things (edge-scroll, labels placed below a point via `below()`) must account for `yaw`.
- Hit chance shown per shot as "64% · open / cover / heavy", XCOM style: best of the selected soldiers on that enemy; adds "· peek" if the shot needs a lean and "· ambush" while undetected; green ≥60 %, amber ≥35 %, red below; grey "no line" if nobody selected can shoot him.

### Controls
- Fit map button is top right of the map; the sneak/run toggle is bottom right (lifted above the cards when they show).
- Bottom bar: team tabs Alpha / Bravo / All (each with a health pip per soldier), Suppress, Clear. Tap a tab to select that team; tap it again while that group is selected to deselect it (Bryan).
- Unit cards (Bryan's design, after Dark Descent's style of HUD; our own look): float over the bottom of the map for the selected team(s), all four when both teams are selected. Each card: procedural portrait, callsign + name, status word, a visual-only heart monitor (no sound) and equipment (weapon label, grenade button with count). Monitor colour: green ≥70 HP, yellow ≥35, orange below, red when downed (erratic, ~150 bpm), grey flatline when dead; rate ≈ 66 + suppression×55 + injury×0.45 + moving (sneak 8 / run 24) + contact 10. Tap a card to select just him; the cards on show stay as they were (`cardGroup` in state.js, kept by `select()` when one soldier is picked from the group already showing). Tap his card again to go back to the whole group on show (Bryan). Tabs, lasso, tapping a soldier on the map (his team) and Clear reset which cards show; Clear hides them. Bryan wants to test the portraits and judge them.
- One-finger drag on empty ground: pan the map (with glide). Drag starting on a soldier: draw his route. Pinch: zoom 1–4×. Two-finger twist: rotate the camera around the point between the fingers (starts after 0.2 rad of twist so pinches don't turn it; `yaw` in scene.js). Two fingers dragged together orbit the camera: pushing up tilts towards the horizon, pulling down tilts back (inverted on purpose, like Ashvale) (pitch down to 14°; `tilt`, `tiltCam`), left/right spins it round the view centre (`ORBIT_SPIN`), the scene following the fingers. The gesture decides orbit vs pinch/twist from each finger's own movement (both >8 px the same way = orbit; opposite ways or one finger alone = pinch), because touch events arrive one finger at a time. Fit map button resets zoom, rotation and tilt. Taps that don't land on the map (beyond its edge) are ignored.
- Tap a soldier: select his fire team. Hold a soldier (380 ms): select just him.
- Lasso select: hold empty ground (380 ms), then draw a loop round soldiers (freeform, closed automatically; `inLoop`). There is no Box button (Bryan).
- Tap a visible enemy: focus fire (before contact: open fire, which springs the ambush). Suppress button then tap an enemy: pin that area. Grenade: tap the grenade on a soldier's card (1 each, 170 range), tap again to cancel. Tap a downed teammate with a soldier selected: go revive.
- Keyboard: Space pause, Esc clear, 1/2 select teams, R sneak/run toggle, Q/E rotate camera 15°, wheel zoom, Shift+wheel tilt.

### Rules and numbers (first guesses, not tuned)
- Hit chance: 0.86 − dist/900; half cover −0.28, heavy −0.42; shooter moving −0.15, target moving −0.08; suppression up to −0.25; enemies −0.10. Clamped 5–95 %.
- Bursts of 3, every 1.6–2.2 s, range 380. Damage 11–16 per hit (enemies ×0.6).
- Suppression: +0.06 per incoming shot, +0.17 per suppress-fire shot, decays 0.18/s. Pinned above 0.65: fires at 40 % rate, −0.25 accuracy, enemies stop repositioning.
- Cover = an object within 15 of the target that the shot line crosses. Full objects (containers, walls, truck, racks, fence) block line of sight; half cover (sandbags, crates, barrels, pallets, barrier, window sills) doesn't.
- Peeking round corners (both sides): if a full object within 22 blocks the line, a standing soldier can lean 8 or 14 sideways past its edge to see, shoot, suppress or throw (`leanPts`/`sightFrom` in map.js, `fireLine` in combat.js). After firing or throwing from a lean he stays leaned out 1.2 s (`PEEK_T`): enemies can see and shoot that spot, and he counts as half cover there. Moving cancels it. Cover scoring for moves counts a corner you can peek from as a spot that "sees" the enemy. UI: pill adds "· peek", shield turns amber, row reads Peeking.
- Downed players bleed out in 45 s; a teammate standing still within 24 revives in 3 s at 35 hp.
- Stealth (first pass, Bryan wants to discuss further): gait is automatic, sneak (24/s, low pose) while undetected with a live enemy within 300, run (54/s) otherwise (`gaitOf`). Unaware enemies build awareness 0..1 instead of spotting instantly (`sense` in combat.js): seen in the ±62° cone within 300 (or within 80 any direction at half rate) adds 1.6×(1−d/330)/s × running 1.3 / sneaking 0.6 / still in the open 0.45 / still in cover 0.3. Running within 110 is heard through walls (+0.5×(1−d/110)/s). Fades 0.15/s. At 0.35 he turns towards what he noticed ("?" callout); at 1 he raises the alarm for everyone. Gunfire and grenades still alert at once. Example spot times: 10 m in the open 2.1 s still / 1.6 s sneaking / 0.7 s running.
- Detection indicator top centre (`#alert`, `alertMeter` in hud.js): ring fills clockwise from the top with the most aware enemy, starting faint and see-through and getting more solid, redder, brighter and glowing as it fills; the filled arc throbs past 70 %, shows red "!!" once spotted. View cones redden with awareness.
- Enemies: cone vision (±62°, 300) until alerted, then reposition inside the warehouse when exposed, fall back below 40 hp.
- Second wave (Bryan's design): when the warehouse is cleared, "All clear!" (grey-blue) fades in and out for 2.6 s; then a red 5 s countdown in hundredths (5.00 → 0.00) with "INCOMING!" above it pulses each whole second where the detection ring sits (ring hidden); at zero "Enemy inbound!" (red) flashes the same way and a gun jeep drives in through the north gate (60/s) and parks at (475,190). On parking it becomes a half-cover object (`addObstacle` in pathfinding.js: nav cells and cover points updated, undone by `removeObstacle` on reset), its gunner (E5, `gunner`, stands at elev 9 in the turret, never moves, always visible) opens up, and four hostiles (E6–E9, `wave:2`) dismount. Gunner: 5-round bursts every 1.3–1.8 s, range 440, −0.05 accuracy, +0.08 suppression per shot, damage ×0.45. Wave-two hostiles push (`assaultThink`): hold if they have a covered shot within 240, else move to a cover point within 130 that makes progress towards the nearest soldier (never within 70 of him), every 3–5 s. Clearing wave two shows "All clear!" again, then mission complete. While counting down, `threats()` defaults to the jeep's parking spot so previews judge cover against the north. Timings use game time. Balance is a first guess: with no orders, a fire team in the jeep's open view gets overrun.
- Mission complete (Bryan's design): after wave two's "All clear!", "Mission complete!" (white with a gold glow), then the after-action report: one row per soldier with portrait, rank and level, stats (kills, best streak, revives, grenades, survived / downed / KIA) and an XP bar that fills (and flashes "Level up" / "Promoted"). XP: 40 per kill, 25 per kill beyond the first in the best streak (kills ≤8 s apart), 50 per revive, 15 per grenade, 60 for surviving; KIA soldiers earn nothing (lost). Level n→n+1 costs 100+15(n−1) XP. Ranks every 10 levels, Bryan's names: Private, Private First Class, Corporal, Sergeant, Gunnery Sergeant, Master Sergeant, First Sergeant, Master Gunnery Sergeant, First Major, Master Major, Commander (100+); I suggested an all-enlisted alternative (Staff Sgt, Sgt First Class, Master Sgt, First Sgt, Sergeant Major, Command Sergeant Major, Legend) and he hasn't picked yet. Level gives +0.2 % aim and −0.3 % suppression build-up per level (`rankAim`, `rankNerve`). Progress is saved in localStorage (`warehouseYard.squad.v1`); the intro card has a reset link. Failure still shows the old end screen, no XP.
- Temporary HQ (after the report's Continue): first the camera eases to the captured jeep on the motor pool with "New vehicle unlocked!" (green) for 3 s (`UNLOCK_SHOW`), then a popup over the map (light backdrop, game paused until "Got it"), camera eases to the warehouse centre, status chip "HQ secured". Medical box (195,322 52×44) heals 8 HP/s; supply box (368,322) restores the grenade after 4 s inside; the captured jeep is moved onto the motor-pool box (458,560 44×70) with a full green bar. 30 s in, a red pulsing arrow on the ground in the NE (plus a screen-edge pointer if off view) shows where the next area will be. Free time; no defence/raids at temporary HQs (only if a mobile defence command is built later).
- Jeep as a vehicle (Bryan's design, `vehicle.js`; owned once the HQ opens, `owner:'P'`): 4 seats, one squad. Select soldiers on foot and tap the jeep to board: they walk over; the first to arrive drives, the second is put on the gun (`gunner`, elev 9, uses the machine-gun rules), the rest ride and fight too (Bryan: everyone but the driver shoots): riders sit visible at elev 4 in the front passenger seat and on the back bench, fire their rifles (−0.15 while the jeep moves) and can be shot, with partial cover; the driver is hidden, doesn't shoot, and hits on him go to the jeep. Tapping the jeep with nobody on foot selected selects its crew; any crew in the selection makes ground taps a drive order, two taps like a squad move (ghost jeep + route, "TAP AGAIN TO DRIVE"). Vehicle routes use a second grid `vblk` where a cell is free only with 16 clearance (`findPath(...,VEH)`); a parked jeep is a half-cover obstacle, a moving one isn't. Speed 80, turns 3 rad/s and slows for sharp turns. Tapping a hostile: the gunner takes him and if he has no line the driver moves to the nearest spot 15–34 m away with a clear line (`engageSpot`, retried every 3 s). The sneak toggle becomes a Dismount button (key R too) while crew are selected; dismounting stops the jeep and puts them beside it; if the driver leaves someone else takes the wheel. Damage: enemy bullets aimed at the driver hit the jeep (×0.35) with a 25 % chance of also wounding someone inside (×0.5); the gunner is exposed; crew inside count as partial cover; grenades within 60 hurt it. Smoke under 60 %, fire under 30 %; at 0 it breaks down, the crew bail out and run ~8 m clear on their own, and it explodes 6 s later. A mine (`mines` list, none placed yet) or `vehDestroy(j,'rpg')` blows it up at once: crew still inside are thrown out downed (revivable). Wrecks are charred, smoulder, and stay as cover. Repairs at 6 %/s while parked on the motor-pool box, but not under fire (no enemy seen for 5 s, `FIGHT_QUIET`). Parked at the HQ facing north. Gunner on dismount (Bryan): with the squad selected, Dismount leaves the gunner on the gun ("Covering you!") while the fight is on and he climbs down by himself after 5 s with no enemy in sight; selecting just him and pressing Dismount forces him out; after a dismount only the soldiers who got out stay selected so the next tap moves them (Bryan), and with nobody at the wheel ground taps move whoever in the selection is on foot; breakdowns/explosions empty the jeep. With nobody driving it can't move (a fixed MG nest). HQ patrol: the first time the whole living squad is aboard, four hostiles (E10–E13, `wave:2` push AI) come through the north gate with "Enemy inbound!"; clearing them shows "All clear!" and returns to free time (once only; no report for it). Condition bar and crew count float above it.
- Shipping depot (Bryan's design, reworked to his layout: a south-to-north main road on the right side of the map that you drive up). Same rainy night. From the yard gate an access road (y 710–760, `ACCESS`) runs east to the main road (x 1025–1075, `MAIN`), which runs north from the south fence. Up the main road is the checkpoint, facing south (`CKPT` y 362): a striped boom barrier across the road (decor, not an obstacle: `decor` in map.js, `dir:'x'`), a guard tower on its west side (`booth`, raised concrete sentry hut with a lit window), a watchtower on its east side (`tower`, legs + railed platform + roof + ladder, a searchlight down the road), a sandbagged firing position south-west of it, and three fuel tanks behind it to the north-east with barrels and a crate. Past the checkpoint the road forks at `FORK` (1050,210) into two branches running to the north fence (`BRANCH`): the left one will lead to a large office building, the right one to a raised metal drawbridge (Bryan: to come after the checkpoint; the ground beside the branches is kept clear; the map will probably need to grow north for them). Rows of single and double-stacked containers (`stack:2`, 52 tall) line both sides of the main road south of the checkpoint, with aisles for flanking. Two extra lamp posts (the checkpoint, the road junction) plus the searchlight. Unlock (hq.js): when the HQ patrol dies, after "All clear!" the gate is removed as an obstacle (`openArea`) and slides open, "New area unlocked!" (green), the game pauses while the camera sweeps out to the checkpoint and back (~4 s), then a banner sends you east and up the main road. The red arrow points east at the gate. Camera: its distance is fitted to the yard's size (`FIT`); `VB` is the area it may roam (yard, then the whole map); `lockArea` on reset puts it all back.
- Checkpoint fight (`depot.js`, Bryan's design): when the depot opens, phase `depot`, a new mission (stats and time reset) and a garrison of nine, unaware but jumpy (awareness ×1.3): all facing south: a sentry inside the guard tower (elev 14, heavy cover) and a lookout on the watchtower (elev 41, half cover via `cover:1`) who never move (`post`), two at the boom gate, two at the sandbags, the RPG gunner beside the guard tower, and a two-man patrol strolling the main road south (26/s) to the access-road junction and back with 2.5 s pauses. Alerted, the garrison shifts between cover inside `CK_ZONE`. Soldiers inside a structure see out of it and can be seen (`los` ignores the structure containing either end). RPG (`RPG`): aims 1.3 s at the nearest soldier (the jeep if crewed) with an always-visible red reticle (blast radius) and "RPG!" banner, then a slow rocket (170/s, smoke trail) lands 18–42 off the aim point or on the first solid thing in its way; blast 40, 85 damage, pins; within 30 of the jeep destroys it; reloads 8 s. Test: a soldier who moves at the warning took no damage in 4/4 shots, one who stood took 15–49. Fuel tanks: 100 HP; tap a tank with soldiers selected to have them shoot it (~25 per burst, `focusTank`); a blast within 25 sets one off outright, 25–45 damages it; going up = big blast (140 damage, falling off over ~100, LOS), destroys the jeep within 85, chains to neighbours in ~0.5 s, alarm, then burns 30 s and smokes. Clearing the garrison: "All clear!", "Mission complete!", a report for the checkpoint (fresh stats), then the second HQ (`startHQ2`): popup "Checkpoint secured", medical and supply points west of the road behind the checkpoint, end of the demo area. Before anyone is spotted in the depot, previews judge cover against the checkpoint. From cover ~25 m west of it with no orders, the squad downed 4/9 in 90 s with nobody lost; the guard-tower sentry does most of the damage.
- Saving (Bryan's design, `save.js`, localStorage `warehouseYard.save.v1`): automatic saves at three milestones: warehouse secured (`startHQ`), depot unlocked (`revealArea`), checkpoint secured (`startHQ2`); a green "Saved · …" chip flashes top left. Stored: milestone, each soldier's HP and grenades and whether he's dead, the jeep's position, heading, HP and whether it's a wreck. The dead stay dead unless you start a New game; wounds are kept; a soldier down at the moment of saving is stored at 5 HP. The intro screen shows "Continue · <milestone>" above "New game" when a save exists; the failure screen offers "Retry from last save" and "New game". Continuing rebuilds the milestone directly (enemies already beaten are marked dead and hidden; the lost are `gone`: no body, KIA on their card). New game clears the save; XP and ranks (progress.js) are kept either way.
- Pathfinding: A* on a 10-unit grid with string-pulling (iteration cap 20000 for the 130x90 grid). Cover points are generated around every object edge (merged if closer than 6).
- Spacing between soldiers' destinations: 20 in the open (`SPACE_OPEN`), 10 when both are at cover points (`SPACE_COVER`), so two can share a short bit of cover like the single sandbags. A crowded soldier's HP bar is lifted so the two don't overlap.

### Known gaps / next ideas
- Balance untested at real speed. Bryan's playtests matter more than the numbers above.
- Wave-one enemy AI only shifts and falls back; wave two pushes from cover to cover. Nobody flanks deliberately or throws grenades yet.
- Agreed stealth roadmap (Bryan approved): alert ladder unaware → suspicious → searching (walks to check, stays jumpy) → alerted; alerts spread by shout (~25 m, sight/hearing) and a ~5 s radio call that can be cut short by killing him; break contact after ~30 s unseen → back to searching; light/dark affects spotting; noise from gunfire/doors; bodies found → search + radio; tools: suppressed pistol, takedown from behind, distraction throw, shooting out lights.
- Phone performance of the 3D lighting is unverified, and the depot added 2 point lights, a spotlight, ~40 objects and a bigger ground texture (2080x1440). If it stutters: drop the 4 flashlight spotlights first, then shadow map size (2048 → 1024), then the ground texture scale (`GS`).
- Bryan plans to add a second squad of 4 once the one-squad mechanics feel right.
- Mines on the way to the next area are still an open option (Bryan picked the patrol first).
- Later idea (Bryan): fallen soldiers carried back can be put on life support (a makeshift bed with medical equipment) as an unlockable skill/tool. Not in this demo; for now KIA soldiers are lost.

### Testing note
Headless browsers here have no GPU, so WebGL runs in software at ~3 fps: fine for screenshots and checking layout, useless for judging pacing. For local tests without network, install `three@0.128.0` from npm and serve `build/three.min.js` for the CDN URL (e.g. Playwright `page.route`), or swap the CDN tag for a local copy. Because the game state is global, tests can drive it straight from `page.evaluate` (`update(1/60)`, `tap(...)`, `units`), and seeding `Math.random` makes runs repeatable.
