# Warehouse Yard — squad tactics prototype

Combat demo for Bryan's next game after Ashvale: modern squad tactics (Full Spectrum Warrior squad control + Aliens: Dark Descent look and campaign feel) with base building and async PvP planned later. This demo only tests the battle layer.

## The game idea (long term)
- Main HQ: level up, research weapons, build defenses, store vehicles, collect resources. Modern setting, human vs human.
- Missions: overlay of enemy territory, scouting options, pick the first target, take it, then push to the next outpost/checkpoint. Captured areas become sub-bases (recruit locals, repair gear, heal the wounded, build makeshift tech). Clear the region, upgrade HQ, continue the war.
- Later: async online raids. Players build base defenses, and their forces defend on their own when attacked.
- Visual target: close to Aliens: Dark Descent.

## The demo (warehouse-yard.html)
`warehouse-yard.html` is the page shell (markup only). Three.js r128 from cdnjs (global `THREE`) renders the 3D world; a 2D canvas overlay on top draws the HUD bits (hp bars, hit %, previews, tracers, rain). No build step: open the HTML directly or serve the folder statically.

### Code layout
Styles in `css/style.css`. Game code in `js/` as plain `<script>` files (not ES modules, so it still runs from `file://`). They share one global scope, so load order matters and is set in the HTML:

2. `map.js` map objects (`objs`), lamp/post data, LOS, cover, free-space queries
3. `pathfinding.js` nav grid, A* + string-pulling, window vault links, sight-aware squad routes, cover points
4. `scene.js` `initGL()` (renderer, scene, camera), camera maths (`toS`/`toW`), textures, world meshes, lights, soldier models, `render()`
5. `state.js` battle state variables, `mk`, `reset`, `timeScale`
6. `combat.js` hit chance, shots, suppression, grenades, move assignment, enemy and squad AI, `update(dt)`
7. `hunt.js` enemies that hunt and call it in: local hearing (`alertOut`/`alertOne`/`shout`), last known positions and the hunt (`huntThink`, `giveUp`), flanking (`flankThink`), radio men and reinforcements (`addRadioKit`, `radioTick`, `callIn`, `reinfTick`), barks (`bark`), guard dogs (`dog`, `dogTick`), the call/reinforcement countdown (`threatHud` every frame), contact ending (`huntTick` from `update`)

Only `main.js` does work at load time beyond building pure data (map, nav grid, cover points); everything touching THREE or wiring events runs from boot.

### Decisions made (keep unless Bryan changes them)
- Real-time with slow-mo (quarter speed) while giving orders, plus Pause. Not turn-based.
- Squad is the main unit, split into two 2-man fire teams. Individuals are for special moves (flank, grenade, revive).
- Group move = two taps: first tap previews ghost positions (green = in cover, red = exposed, from known enemies) and the route each man will take, tap the marker again to go. Single soldier moves on one tap.
- Vaulting through windows and over sandbags and barriers (Bryan: windows when it's the quickest or safest way in; sandbags: "jump over sandbags or walk through from either side"): they're links in the squad's pathfinding (`VAULTABLE`, `buildVaultLinks`, `vaultLinks`, `WINDOWS` in pathfinding.js, which despite the name holds every vaultable object: free cells straight across one where a body fits over it), costing their length plus `VAULT_EXTRA` (4.5 cells ≈ the climb's extra time). The climb: within `VAULT_HALF` (13) of the window's middle he moves at `VAULT_SPEED` (20/s) with a hop of `VAULT_H` (10) over the sill, leaning forward, card status "Vaulting", no firing; about 1.3 s. Waypoints after a vault carry `.vault`; route lines and previews draw a little arc over the window (`vaultMark` in overlay.js). The windows' glass is broken out (jagged bits left). The enemy doesn't vault (its moves use `FOOT`). Usable windows: both warehouse windows.
- Safest routes: the squad's moves (`SQUAD` nav, the default) also try a route that keeps out of what known enemies (seen, at their last known spots) would actually spot them in: an unaware one's view cone (±62°, 300, the red wedge on the map) or within 80; an alerted one all round within 380, counting less further off (Bryan found the first version, any line of sight within 38 m, made funny detours near the canal). Sight is a quick check on a grid of the sight-blocking objects (`gridSight`): each cell costs ×(1+n), n capped at 3 (machine guns count 1.5), vaults included. That route is taken if it's at most 1.3× the quickest + 30 (`SAFE_DETOUR`). A tap on water (canal, dock) means the bank on the soldiers' side (`nearBank` in combat.js), so nobody gets sent across. Before any enemy is seen, routes are simply the quickest. Drawn routes fill their gaps without detours (`TRAIL`).
- Where soldiers stand (`makeAssign`, reworked after Bryan's complaints about far-off cover and runners): spots within 14 of the tap for one soldier, 32 for two, 45 for three or four, and in line of sight of the tap (same side of a wall). Candidates are cover points plus open slots in a line across the threat direction (back row penalised). The whole group's arrangement is chosen together: cover (×2) + sees enemy (0.3) − distance from tap/12 − walk/150 − spread beyond 30, and health counts (Bryan: the healthier man goes in front; a weak soldier at a wall's edge kept going down and being revived): each spot's exposure (sees or peeks at the enemy: 1, not fully covered: 1 more) × (his HP − the group's average)/50, and −0.3 more per exposure for anyone under 45 HP in a group, so the healthiest takes the corner. Tapping a wall/container/crate/sandbag in the 3D view targets the spot just in front of the face you tapped (`tapTarget` in scene.js, using `objHeight` from map.js), not the ground hidden behind it.
- Drawn routes for independent soldiers (Bryan, inspired by mobile squad games): drag from a soldier to draw his route. He follows the line's shape; stretches through objects are routed round (`routeFromTrail` in combat.js, dropping a dead-end bit of line if that saves 15%+ locally); the end settles on cover within 18. Quarter speed while drawing, works while paused, map edge-scrolls while drawing.
- Sneak/run toggle (Bryan's design): button bottom right (`#gaitBtn`, `toggleGait`) shows the selected soldiers' gait (AUTO SNEAK / AUTO RUN). Pressing flips them to the other gait (amber, MANUAL), pressing again returns to auto. A soldier goes back to auto when he's selected again after not being selected (`select()` in input.js; always change the selection through it). Key R.
- Weapons tight while undetected: before contact the squad only fires at a hostile you tapped (or via Suppress/grenade). Striking first is an ambush: enemies need 1.4–2 s to react and the squad gets +0.15 hit chance for 1.5 s (`alertAll(msg,true)`, `ambushing()`). The hit pill shows "· ambush" while undetected. After contact, everyone fires freely.
- Soldiers act on their own to survive: return fire, shift to cover within 50 if exposed ~2 s, but never wander off on a plan of their own. Player orders lock out auto-moves for 4 s.
- Angled 3D camera, from the south by default (pitch 60° zoomed out → 46° zoomed in), rotatable by twisting. Bryan liked this far more than top-down. Screen-relative things (edge-scroll, labels placed below a point via `below()`) must account for `yaw`.
- Hit chance shown per shot as "64% · open / cover / heavy", XCOM style: best of the selected soldiers on that enemy; adds "· peek" if the shot needs a lean and "· ambush" while undetected; green ≥60 %, amber ≥35 %, red below; grey "no line" if nobody selected can shoot him.

### Controls
- Fit map button is top right of the map; the sneak/run toggle is bottom right (lifted above the cards when they show).
- Bottom bar: team tabs Alpha / Bravo / All (each with a health pip per soldier), Watch, Breach (only inside the office building or at the warehouse), Suppress, Clear. Tap a tab to select that team; tap it again while that group is selected to deselect it (Bryan).
- Unit cards (Bryan's design, after Dark Descent's style of HUD; our own look): float over the bottom of the map for the selected team(s), all four when both teams are selected. Each card: procedural portrait, callsign + name, status word, a visual-only heart monitor (no sound), the weapon (Bryan, like Dark Descent: our own silhouette drawing of the rifle (a shotgun one too, for later) with its name, `GUN_SVG`; picture only when four cards show) and equipment: a grenade and his special item, with counts (Bryan: every soldier carries a grenade and one special item, smoke, a medic kit or a silencer; one medic kit per fire team to start, A2 and B2, and A1 brings the silencer so it can be tried on the warehouse, `DEFAULT_SPEC` in state.js; in a supply box the item shows ⇄ and a tap swaps it for the next kind, refilled; `SPECIALS`/`SPEC_ORDER`/`specFull` in cards.js, `u.spec`/`u.specN`; more than four cards (the freed prisoners joined) show as two rows of small cards without the monitor, `compact`; the C4 button is off for this level, `C4_ON` in cards.js). Monitor colour: green ≥70 HP, yellow ≥35, orange below, red when downed (erratic, ~150 bpm), grey flatline when dead; rate ≈ 66 + suppression×55 + injury×0.45 + moving (sneak 8 / run 24) + contact 10. Tap a card to select just him; the cards on show stay as they were (`cardGroup` in state.js, kept by `select()` when one soldier is picked from the group already showing). Tap his card again to go back to the whole group on show (Bryan). Tabs, lasso, tapping a soldier on the map (his team) and Clear reset which cards show; Clear hides them. Bryan wants to test the portraits and judge them.
- One-finger drag on empty ground: pan the map (with glide). The drag is measured at the screen's centre (always ground), capped per move, and the glide uses the drag's speed over its last ~0.1 s, capped at 1400/zoom per s (Bryan's "camera flies off": touch events 1 ms apart made the old per-event speed huge, and drags near the horizon mapped to huge distances); pinch and wheel zoom only re-anchor on ground near the view (`nearGround`). Drag starting on a soldier: draw his route. Pinch: zoom 1–4×. Two-finger twist: rotate the camera around the point between the fingers (starts after 0.2 rad of twist so pinches don't turn it; `yaw` in scene.js). Two fingers dragged together orbit the camera: pushing up tilts towards the horizon, pulling down tilts back (inverted on purpose, like Ashvale) (pitch down to 14°; `tilt`, `tiltCam`), left/right spins it round the view centre (`ORBIT_SPIN`), the scene following the fingers. The gesture decides orbit vs pinch/twist from each finger's own movement (both >8 px the same way = orbit; opposite ways or one finger alone = pinch), because touch events arrive one finger at a time. Fit map button resets zoom, rotation and tilt. Taps that don't land on the map (beyond its edge) are ignored.
- Tap a soldier: select his fire team. With several selected, tapping one of them selects just him, like his card (the cards on show stay), and tapping him again goes back to the group (Bryan). Hold a soldier (380 ms): select just him.
- Lasso select: hold empty ground (380 ms), then draw a loop round soldiers (freeform, closed automatically; `inLoop`). There is no Box button (Bryan).
- Tap a visible enemy: focus fire (before contact: open fire, which springs the ambush). Suppress button then tap an enemy: pin that area. Grenade: tap the grenade on a soldier's card (1 each, 170 range), tap again to cancel. Smoke (a special item; Bryan, in C4's place for this level): tap it on his card (170 range, restocked at supply boxes), then inside the circle: a cloud about 12 m across for 14 s that nobody sees through either way (from 0.4 s until it thins at 12 s; not movement; `SMOKE`/`smokes`/`smokeBlocks` in map.js, checked by `los`; `throwSmoke`/`popSmoke` in combat.js; `drawSmoke` in overlay.js; quiet, no alarm). Aiming never moves anyone (Bryan): a suppress/grenade tap that misses keeps the mode and says why; drags while aiming pan instead of drawing a route; for 1.2 s after the order map taps are ignored, and for 6 s even a single soldier needs the two-tap move (`fireOrderAt` in input.js). A soldier suppressing, or within 6 s of throwing, doesn't auto-shift to cover (`playerThink`). Tap a downed teammate with a soldier selected: go revive (the first 60 s). Once he's critical the tap sends a selected soldier who has a medic kit to use it, or else the nearest selected to carry him (`orderMedkit`/`orderCarry`). A medic kit can also be aimed: tap the kit on his card, then a downed teammate (or his carrier).
- Silent takedown (Bryan): with one soldier selected, an unaware hostile who's visible, within 14 m (`TK_RANGE`) and has his back to him (the soldier outside his view cone; not a sentry inside a structure) shows TAKEDOWN instead of the hit chance, and the hint line says so. Tapping him sends the soldier sneaking up to a free spot at his back (`behindSpot`: straight behind, else round to either side; forced sneak; re-paths every 0.3 s if the man moves or turns), then he grabs him (0.6 s, the man frozen: `grabbed`) and kills him silently (`silentKill`: no shot, no blast, no alarm, so the quiet spell isn't reset; counts as a kill for XP). The target keeps the TAKEDOWN label and gets a green dashed ring; card "Sneaking up" / "Takedown". If he's alerted on the way in, it's off and the soldier stops; another order cancels it. `canTakedown`/`orderTakedown`/`takedownTick` in combat.js; a tap goes to it before focus fire (input.js). Bodies can be found (layers.js). In a test, two of the four warehouse guards went down in a row from behind without the others noticing: may be too easy, Bryan to judge.
- Silencer (Bryan: a special item that wears out): while it lasts (6 to start, topped up at supply boxes; each burst, suppressing shot or silent shot uses one) the soldier's gunfire is heard only within 9 m in sight or 4 m through walls (not the whole room inside) and shows no muzzle flash. With just him selected, a hostile who hasn't noticed the squad, in his sight within 20 m, shows SILENT SHOT and the chance (an aimed shot, +0.2; TAKEDOWN wins if both work): tap him and he takes aim for 0.45 s and fires once. A hit drops the man without a sound (a body that can still be found); a miss alerts him and anyone right by him. Tapping the silencer on his card readies the same thing (mode 'silent'). `SIL`, `silenced`, `canSilent`, `orderSilent`, `silentTick` in combat.js.
- Watch (Full Spectrum Warrior's fire sector; Bryan: they take cover if it's there): press Watch, then tap where to watch. Each selected soldier takes the nearest cover within 40 that still sees that way and holds, facing it; a hostile coming into his cone (±36°, 30 m, a dashed wedge that turns amber with someone in it) gets a snap shot at once, +0.15 to hit. It's an order to fire: even before contact, a hostile walking into the cone is shot (an ambush). Any move order ends it. Breach room (Bryan: a similar order): press Breach, then tap a room (inside the office building, or the warehouse); the team stacks up either side of the room's nearest doorway (BREACH · STACKING UP), then goes in together to spots either side inside: +0.2 to hit for 2.5 s, weapons free into the room, and anyone inside who hadn't seen them is slow to react (0.9 s). `orders.js`; card statuses Watching, Stacking up, Breaching.
- Keyboard: Space pause, Esc clear, 1/2 select teams, R sneak/run toggle, Q/E rotate camera 15°, wheel zoom, Shift+wheel tilt.

### Rules and numbers (first guesses, not tuned)
- Hit chance: 0.86 − dist/900; half cover −0.28, heavy −0.42; shooter moving −0.15, target moving −0.08; suppression up to −0.25; enemies −0.10. Clamped 5–95 %.
- Weapons: everyone carries an assault rifle for now (Bryan tried two shotguns, then took them out; `SHOTGUNNERS` is empty, the shotgun code and sound stay for later). Rifle: bursts of 3, every 1.6–2.2 s, range 380, damage 11–16 per hit (enemies ×0.6). Shotgun (dormant): one blast (4 pellet tracers) every 1.1–1.5 s, range 200 (`wRange`), hit chance 1 within 80 then −1/150 per unit beyond, cover counts less (−0.2 half / −0.3 heavy), damage 38–50 within 60 falling to 35 % at 260, +0.14 suppression per blast; recorded Benelli shotgun sound. Against an enemy in half cover: 6 m shotgun 80 % / rifle 51 %; 15 m shotgun 33 %; 25 m shotgun out of range, rifle 30 %. Enemies all carry rifles.
- Suppression: +0.06 per incoming shot, +0.17 per suppress-fire shot, decays 0.18/s. Pinned above 0.65: fires at 40 % rate, −0.25 accuracy, enemies stop repositioning.
- Cover = an object within 15 of the target that the shot line crosses. Full objects (containers, walls, truck, racks) block line of sight; half cover (sandbags, crates, barrels, pallets, barrier, window sills) doesn't. Chain-link fences and gates block movement only: sight, shots, throws and blasts go through (Bryan: he couldn't see or throw through the depot's fence; they're not in `FULL`).
- Peeking round corners (both sides): if a full object within 22 blocks the line, a standing soldier can lean 8 or 14 sideways past its edge to see, shoot, suppress or throw (`leanPts`/`sightFrom` in map.js, `fireLine` in combat.js). After firing or throwing from a lean he stays leaned out 1.2 s (`PEEK_T`): enemies can see and shoot that spot, and he counts as half cover there. Moving cancels it. Cover scoring for moves counts a corner you can peek from as a spot that "sees" the enemy. UI: pill adds "· peek", shield turns amber, row reads Peeking.
- Downed (Bryan: "a 60 second leeway, then make it difficult"): for 60 s (`BLEED_T`) a teammate standing still within 24 revives him in 3 s at 35 hp, but not while that teammate is pinned (`PINNED`, 0.65; the label reads HELPER PINNED). Then he's critical for 2 min (`CRIT_T`; KIA after that, a default Bryan may change): no hand revive. Two ways to save him: a medic kit (5 s beside him, not while pinned; up at 50 HP; `MEDKIT_T`, `medTick`), or carrying him (1 s to pick up; the carrier moves at sneaking speed, can't shoot or climb, a route with a vault is redone without one, and Put down takes the gait button's place; carried into a medical box he's set down there and is back up 3 s later at 35 HP, `BOXREV_T`; a carrier who goes down drops him; he goes in and out of the office with his carrier). Labels: CRITICAL · m:ss, CARRIED · m:ss, MEDIC KIT / MEDIC PINNED; card statuses Critical, Carried, Carrying, Medic kit. A revived soldier keeps his head down for 5 s, and so does one under 40 HP who's just been hit (4 s; `shy`, `SHY_HP`, `SHY_T`): no leaning out to shoot or look, so enemies can't hit him at a corner.
- Stealth (first pass, Bryan wants to discuss further): gait is automatic, sneak (24/s, low pose) while undetected with a live enemy within 300, run (54/s) otherwise (`gaitOf`). Unaware enemies build awareness 0..1 instead of spotting instantly (`sense` in combat.js): seen in the ±62° cone within 300 adds 1.6×(1−d/330)/s × running 1.3 / sneaking 0.6 / still in the open 0.45 / still in cover 0.3; within 80 outside the cone (behind him) it's × running 0.65 / sneaking 0.04 / still 0.03, so you can creep up behind a man for a takedown but not run up on him. Running within 110 is heard through walls (+0.5×(1−d/110)/s). Fades 0.15/s. At 0.35 he turns towards what he noticed ("?" callout); at 1 he raises the alarm for everyone. Gunfire and grenades still alert at once. Example spot times: 10 m in the open 2.1 s still / 1.6 s sneaking / 0.7 s running.

### Testing note
Headless browsers here have no GPU, so WebGL runs in software at ~3 fps: fine for screenshots and checking layout, useless for judging pacing. For local tests without network, install `three@0.128.0` from npm and serve `build/three.min.js` for the CDN URL (e.g. Playwright `page.route`), or swap the CDN tag for a local copy. Because the game state is global, tests can drive it straight from `page.evaluate` (`update(1/60)`, `tap(...)`, `units`), and seeding `Math.random` makes runs repeatable. To test inside the office building, store a 'dco' save and call `continueGame()`, put the squad in `DC_ENTER` and `interiorTap(245,-410)`; the fade runs on real-time timeouts and `requestAnimationFrame`, so wait about a second with the frame loop running. Things inside are driven through `ACTS` (find one by id: 'computer', 'card', 'stairs-GFB1', 'lift-GF', 'liftdoor', 'cell-C1', 'cutdown') and `orderUse(a)` with soldiers selected; the story's state is in `office`.
