# Zombie apocalypse (shelved idea)

Status: shelved. A first level draft ("Day One", 2026-11-22) was built and scrapped: Bryan didn't like it. Start the next attempt fresh.

## Pitch
An end-of-the-world zombie game built on the Full Spectrum engine (the squad tactics battle layer from `warehouse-yard.html`). One unit at the start, at home in the suburbs, waking up in bed to the news on TV. It starts as survival through the first attack, then grows into team building, base building and scavenging.

## Bryan's decisions so far
- **Opening feel:** think of a classic zombie film's opening: a normal suburban morning that falls apart fast.
- **Zombies:** both runners and shamblers.
- **Tone:** serious mixed with over-the-top.
- **Death:** leaning toward dead units staying dead.
- **Structure:** a survival game, with story and sandbox elements. How they mix is still open.
