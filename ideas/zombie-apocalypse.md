# Zombie apocalypse (shelved idea)

Status: shelved, 2026-11-22. A short draft to come back to. Bryan will add detail as the game draft grows.

## Pitch
An end-of-the-world zombie game built on the Full Spectrum engine (the squad tactics battle layer from `warehouse-yard.html`).

## Opening
- You start with one unit, at home in the suburbs.
- He wakes up in bed with the TV news on as the outbreak breaks.
- The first stretch is pure survival: get through the first wave of zombies.

## Progression (rough arc)
1. Survive the initial onslaught alone.
2. Team building: find and recruit other survivors, growing toward a squad.
3. Base building: fortify a home base.
4. Scavenging runs out into the surrounding area for supplies.

## Engine reuse (to look into later)
- Squad control, fire teams, cover and hit chance carry over directly.
- Starting with a single unit means the controls must work with one soldier before teams exist.
- Enemy AI would need a new mode: zombies swarm and push, unlike the cover-based hostiles now.
- Base building and scavenging overlap with the HQ/sub-base plans for the main game.

## Open questions
- Is the tone grounded/serious or more pulpy?
- Do zombies run or shamble?
- How does the run end: a permadeath campaign, a sandbox, or a story?
