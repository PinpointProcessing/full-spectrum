## What changes, in both options

- **The lake opens out to the sea.** Its far shore across the head is gone. North of the Headquarters the land ends in a coast
  just past the moat and runs away west. The hill comes down into the water at its north end. The summit (Level 4's) stays where
  it is.
- **The water goes on past where anything is drawn.** The far water fades into a pale haze, the colour of the sky low down. That
  makes the horizon show at night, and land far off stands out dark against it. A sky with a little light near the horizon takes
  the place of plain black.
- **The lighthouse stands on a large island far out.** The island's east end, with the lighthouse, sits in the gap between the
  Headquarters and the hill. The rest of it runs away west behind the Headquarters, so from the bridge there is open sea to the
  right of it.
  - The lighthouse is taller than before (about 23 m) so it reads at that distance.
  - Its light and beam aren't dimmed by the night air, and the beam is longer.
  - There's a keeper's cottage with one window lit.
  - The little island in the lake goes.
- **The walk across uses the game's own camera** at the lowest angle it goes to (14° over the water). The picture is moved down a
  little, so the horizon and some sky are in it and the squad sits in the lower part of the frame. The squad is the same size on
  screen as now.
