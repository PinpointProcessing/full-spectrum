# Level 4 draft: "Dead Air" (the old radio tower)

Bryan, after v98: "Show me a draft". This is the next mission, the one the road east and the tunnel lead to. The map draft
is `docs/map-draft-H.png`. Nothing here is built: it's for Bryan to change.

## The story

The HQ's radio needs parts. The scientist said the decommissioned radio tower through the tunnel down the road would have
them. The militia (the men from the data center) got there first and use the old transmitter building as a listening
post. And the centipede the squad saw crawl into the tunnel lives in there now.

## The map

- **The tunnel** (its own map, built in v96 to v98): pitch black, the wrecks and the dead. New: the Crawler on its roof,
  eggs in the lorry's trailer, and a breaker room behind the east cross-passage door. A checkpoint at the north end.
- **The valley** out of the north portal: the road east along the valley floor, a militia roadblock on it (two men and a
  dog), then switchbacks up the hill to the compound's gate. North-west of the road, thick woods: cover all the way up to
  the compound's back fence, and the hunters live in them. A burnt farmhouse on the valley floor.
- **The compound** on the hilltop, inside a chain-link fence (see and shoot through it, not walk through it):
  - the gate on the south side, a machine gun just inside it, two men;
  - the dead mast, 40 m, guy wires, a sniper on its platform covering the road up;
  - the transmitter building, two rooms for a Breach: the equipment room (the transmitter) and the office (the radio log,
    and the radio man who calls the truck in);
  - two of their containers (the amplifier), the generator shed (it runs the floodlights), a patrol round the inside of
    the fence with a dog;
  - a gap in the back fence by the transmitter building: the quiet way in, through the woods;
  - their road north, off the map: where their truck comes from.

## How it plays

1. **The tunnel.** The Crawler stalks the squad along the roof in the dark and drops on the last man in line. It knocks
   him down and drags him toward the dark, and the others have a few seconds to shoot it off before it climbs away. Hurt
   enough, it flees north. Eggs in the lorry hatch if anyone goes near. Optional: the breaker behind the east door puts
   the lights back on and drives it off for good.
2. **The valley road.** Take the roadblock (loud, or a quiet volley and the dog), or go round it through the woods (the
   hunters).
3. **The compound.** The sniper on the mast first, the machine gun at the gate, the patrol and its dog, the radio man (cut
   his call short or the truck comes: four men down the north road). Quiet way: the gap in the back fence, then Breach the
   transmitter building. Cutting the generator kills the floodlights, like Lights Out.
4. **The supplies.** Three to carry: the transmitter, the amplifier and the cable drum at the mast's foot. One soldier
   carries each, the way a wounded man is carried: slow, no shooting, Put down any time. The radio log is intel. A
   checkpoint once the compound is taken.
5. **Home.** Carry them back down to the tunnel and through it to the road. Gunfire anywhere wakes the woods: the hunters
   come for whoever is loudest, the militia too. Two bonus crates (the farmhouse and the woods) for whoever goes looking.
   At the HQ the supplies go on the supply table: "Mission complete!" and the report.
