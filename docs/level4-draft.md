## The story

The HQ's radio needs parts. The scientist said the decommissioned radio tower "accessible from the tunnel down the road"
would have them: it stands on top of the hill the road tunnel runs under, and the way up is from inside the tunnel.

## The way through, bottom to top

1. **The road tunnel** (built in v96 to v98): over the bridge and in, pitch black, the dead. Its north end has caved in, so
   the way on is the service door in its wall (by a red emergency light).
2. **The plant**: the tunnel's control rooms in the rock above the road (an interior map, like the office building's
   floors). A corridor from the service door; the staff room and the store (two bonus crates); the control room (the
   tunnel's monitors, a dead operator, and the switch that puts the road tunnel's lights back on); the generator room
   (power for the rail car); the fan hall, where the brood has nested (resin, eggs, hatchlings); and the lower station of
   the incline. A checkpoint once it's quiet.
3. **The incline**: a long access tunnel climbing up through the rock to the summit, steps beside a rail track. The
   Crawler, the centipede the squad saw go in, lives in it. Climb the steps and fight it in the narrow tunnel, or start
   the generator and ride the car up (quicker, but it can come for the car).
4. **The summit**: the old radio tower. The incline's top station is a bunker by the mast. A militia outpost holds the
   summit, about 8 men: a sniper on the mast, a machine gun over their own track up the north side, men round the radio
   building. They watch the track and the road below, not the bunker: the squad comes out behind them. Take the
   transmitter (radio building), the amplifier (their containers) and the cable drum (at the mast's foot); the radio log is
   intel. A checkpoint once the summit is taken.
5. **Home**: back down through the hill carrying the supplies (one soldier each, slow, no shooting, the way a wounded man
   is carried), or one call on the old tower's radio: command sends a helicopter to the summit's old helipad and flies the
   squad and the supplies home to the HQ's roof.
